THE STORY OF THE REDS

OUR HISTORY

From a small football club in Munich to one of the greatest clubs in the world.

1900

The Foundation

FC Bayern München was founded on 27 February 1900 at Café Gisela in Munich, led by Franz John.

1932

First German Championship

Bayern won their first German national championship after defeating Eintracht Frankfurt 2-0.

1965

Bundesliga Promotion

Bayern earned promotion to the Bundesliga and began a new chapter in German football.

1974 - 1976

European Dominance

Bayern won three consecutive European Cups, led by legendary players such as Franz Beckenbauer and Gerd Müller.

2001

European Glory Again

Bayern defeated Valencia on penalties to win the UEFA Champions League.

2013

The Historic Treble

Bayern won the Bundesliga, DFB-Pokal, and UEFA Champions League in one season.

2020 - 2021

The Sextuple Year

Bayern completed an incredible year by winning six trophies, including the Champions League and FIFA Club World Cup.
